Information from official sources

Every tip shows its source inside the app: Spanish Civil Protection (national and regional), 112 services, Spanish Red Cross, 2025 resuscitation guidelines and public health agencies. Educational projects are based on universities, science museums and public bodies. Emergency 72h is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these organisations.

This app offers general information and does not replace emergency services. In an emergency, call 112 and follow the authorities' instructions. Always check with a healthcare professional before making medical decisions.
