Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Summary

Emergency 72h does not collect, send or sell any personal data. The app works offline and has no user accounts, servers, analytics or ads.

Data you keep on your phone

Some features store information only on your device:

  • My card: name, blood type, allergies, conditions, medication, notes and emergency contacts that you type in.
  • Emergency kit: which items you ticked, number of people and the date of the last check.
  • Settings: language and battery saver mode.

This information never leaves your phone and nobody else, including the developer, can access it. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete all my data or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to turn on the flash (torch and SOS signal). The app takes no photos or videos.
  • Calls: when you press "Call 112", the app asks for confirmation and then opens the call on your phone. Calling a contact from your card opens your phone. The app does not record your calls.

External links

The official sources cited open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that processes data (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated beforehand and the app will say so.
