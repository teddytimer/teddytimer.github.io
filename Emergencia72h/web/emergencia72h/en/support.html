Frequently asked questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. All content is inside the app. You only need a connection to open links to the official sources.

How does the "Call 112" button work?

When you press it, the app asks "Call 112 now?" to avoid accidental calls. If you confirm, the call opens on your phone. 112 is free.

Why does it ask for camera permission?

Only to turn on the flash for the torch and SOS signal. If you say no, you can use the screen as a light.

Where is "My card" data stored?

Only on your phone. It is never sent anywhere. You can delete it in Settings.

Where does the information come from?

From official sources: Spanish Civil Protection, 112 services, Spanish Red Cross, 2025 resuscitation guidelines and public health agencies. Each guide lists its sources at the end.

I found an outdated tip

Write to us with the guide and the current official source. We review the content in every version.
